Introduction

Paleopathology and archeometrics are disciplines applied for todays studies regarding ancient diseases and injuries in organisms. Both operates by applying chemical and physical analysis to the examination of fossils and quantitative analysis while examining early books, paintings and sculptures from the past.

Our Idea

An ontology about palaeopathological claims. PaleONT does not model the diseases of the people buried in medieval Danish cemeteries. It models what someone said about them on the basis of which observation, with which instrument, within which framework of thought, and with how much stated confidence.

The goal is not to show that paleopathologists contradict one another, that would be a trivial conclusion. We neither want to be considered too skeptical nor too credulous, instead we want to show that every assertion carries with it the identity of who made it, the instruments used, and their underlying assumptions. Therefore, we states that scholarly disagreement is not a system error; it is the diagnostic symptom that makes a specific scientific standpoint visible.
